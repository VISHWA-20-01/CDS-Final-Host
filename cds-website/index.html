Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with CDS.

How quickly can you start my project?

We typically deploy core teams within 2 weeks. Our agile process ensures we hit the ground running without long delays.

Do you work with legacy systems?

Yes. Legacy modernization is one of our core strengths. We specialize in migrating monoliths to secure cloud-native architectures.

How do you handle data security?

Security is built-in via DevSecOps. We comply with SOC2/HIPAA standards from day one to ensure your data is always protected.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. 100%. Once the project is delivered and paid for, all intellectual property rights belong specifically to your company.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Absolutely. We offer flexible SLAs for 24/7 monitoring, security patching, and ongoing feature updates to keep you running smooth.

What is your pricing model?

We offer both Fixed Price (for well-defined scopes) and Time & Materials (for agile/evolving projects) to suit your specific needs.
