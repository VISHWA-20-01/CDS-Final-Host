Cookie Policy

Last Updated: February 08, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Strictly Necessary: Essential for the website to function.
  • Performance: Google Analytics cookies help us understand which "Focus Areas" (e.g., Product Engineering vs. AI) are most popular.
  • Functionality: To remember your region preference (e.g., showing content relevant to the Netherlands vs. Caribbean).

3. Cookie Management

  • Consent Banner: Upon your first visit, you will see a banner allowing you to "Accept" or "Decline" non-essential cookies.
  • Browser Settings: You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some "Glassmorphism" animations or interactive features may degrade.

We update our policies to reflect service and legal changes. We encourage users to check this page frequently for updates.
