Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 08, 2026

1. Introduction

Caribbean Digital Solutions ("CDS", "we", "us") provides digital transformation, AI, and cloud consulting services. We are committed to protecting the data of our clients across the USA, Caribbean, LATAM, and EMEA regions. This policy outlines how we handle your data in compliance with the GDPR (Europe), CCPA (California), and regional Caribbean data standards.

2. Information We Collect

  • Direct Interactions: When you use our "Request Demo" forms or chat widgets, we collect your Name, Email, Job Title, and Company Name.
  • Automated Technologies: As you navigate our "Glassmorphism" interface, we use cookies and server logs to collect IP addresses, browser types, and device data to optimize site performance.
  • Client Data: Information provided during consulting engagements (e.g., Insurance or Banking datasets) is processed strictly according to specific Master Services Agreements (MSA), not this public policy.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Service Delivery: To respond to inquiries regarding our "Core Offerings" (Cloud Migration, AI & Data Science).
  • Optimization: To analyze traffic patterns on our "Impact" and "Case Studies" pages using analytics tools.
  • Communication: To send newsletters or industry insights (only if you opt-in).

4. International Data Transfers

CDS is a global entity. Your data may be processed in the Caribbean, USA, or India by our technical teams.

For EU/EEA Citizens

We transfer data subject to appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), ensuring your rights under GDPR are preserved even when data is accessed by our delivery centers in Mexico or India.

5. Your Rights

  • GDPR (Europe): You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • CCPA (USA): California residents have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (Note: CDS does not sell data).
  • To Exercise Rights: Contact our Compliance Team at sathish@cdsgroups.com.

6. Data Security and Updates

We utilize enterprise-grade security measures, including SSL encryption and secure cloud storage, to protect your information. We update our policies to reflect service and legal changes. We encourage users to check this page frequently for updates.
